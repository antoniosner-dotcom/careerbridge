How does the match score work?
  1. Each course and career is linked to a set of skills.
  2. We check which skills your completed courses already cover.
  3. A course scores higher when it fills bigger gaps in the skills your career needs most.

A score compares courses with each other. It is not a chance of getting a job. In this mockup, scores are random placeholders until the algorithm is built.
